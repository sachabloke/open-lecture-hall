About & rights

These lectures belong to the universities that made them.

Open Lecture Hall is a free, non-commercial index of university lectures that their owners already give away for free. It exists so that people who cannot pay for education can find them, and so that recordings which universities have taken down can still be found in public archives.

What this site does and does not do

  • It does not host any lectures. Every video and recording stays where its owner put it: the university's own website, YouTube, or the Internet Archive. This site links to it, or plays it with the official embedded player that YouTube and the Internet Archive offer for this purpose.
  • It is not run by, connected to, or endorsed by any university. The drawn buildings are cartoons, not the universities' own images, and no crests or logos are used.
  • It is free and non-commercial. There are no adverts, no payments and no sign-up.
  • It keeps nothing about you. The ticks and "My shelf" are stored only in your own browser and are never sent anywhere.

Asking for something to be removed

If you own a lecture or course listed here and would rather it were not, tell us which one and it will be taken off the site. You do not need to give a reason, and no proof beyond saying who you are is needed for a link to be removed.

Send a removal request

This opens a short form on GitHub, where this site is hosted, and needs a free GitHub account. Requests are public, so leave out anything private. Copyright owners can also use GitHub's own copyright notice process, which needs no account.

Corrections

If a link is broken, a course is filed under the wrong subject or university, or a title is wrong, the same form works for that too.

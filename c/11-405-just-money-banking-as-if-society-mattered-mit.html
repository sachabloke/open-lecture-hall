11.405 Just Money: Banking as if Society Mattered

Katrin Käufer, J. Phillip Thompson · 2021 · free course materials

Do you know what your bank does with your money? What is the role of a bank in producing societal well-being? This course looks into banks that operate differently, namely, “just banks" that use capital and finance as a tool to address social and ecological challenges. This course is for anyone…

11.421 Housing and Human Services

Langley Keyes, Martin Rein · 2005 · free course materials

This class focuses on how the housing and human service systems interact: how networks and social capital can build between elements of the two systems. It explores ways in which the differing world views, professional perspectives, and institutional needs of the two systems play out…

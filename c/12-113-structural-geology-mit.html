12.113 Structural Geology

B. Clark Burchfiel, Christopher Studnicki-Gizbert · 2005 · free course materials

Structural geology is the study of processes and products of rock deformation. This course introduces the techniques of structural geology through a survey of the mechanics of rock deformation, a survey of the features and geometries of faults and folds, and techniques of strain analysis.

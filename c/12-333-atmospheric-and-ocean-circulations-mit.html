12.333 Atmospheric and Ocean Circulations

R. Alan Plumb · 2004 · free course materials

In this course, we will look at many important aspects of the circulation of the atmosphere and ocean, from length scales of meters to thousands of km and time scales ranging from seconds to years. We will assume familiarity with concepts covered in course 12.003 (Physics of the Fluid Earth).…

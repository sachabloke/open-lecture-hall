14.310x Data Analysis for Social Scientists

Esther Duflo, Sara Ellison · 2023 · 23 free video lectures

This course introduces methods for harnessing data to answer questions of cultural, social, economic, and policy interest. We will start with essential notions of probability and statistics. We will proceed to cover techniques in modern data analysis: regression and econometrics,…

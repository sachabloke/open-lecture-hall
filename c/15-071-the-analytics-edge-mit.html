15.071 The Analytics Edge

Dimitris Bertsimas · 2017 · 194 free video lectures

This course presents real-world examples in which quantitative methods provide a significant competitive edge that has led to a first order impact on some of today’s most important companies. We outline the competitive landscape and present the key quantitative methods that created the edge…

15.341 Individuals, Groups, and Organizations

John Carroll · 2006 · free course materials

This class develops basic concepts for understanding individual, group, and organizational behavior through the critical analysis of important works in the field. Among the areas covered are: individual affect and cognition; group process and performance; and organizational culture and adaptation.…

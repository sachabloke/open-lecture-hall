15.879 Building Serious System Dynamics Models from Scratch

Hazhir Rahmandad · 2025 · 12 free video lectures

The focus of this advanced research seminar is on building robust system dynamics models from scratch. This class helps students build hands-on modeling skills that allow them to tackle their own modeling projects independently.

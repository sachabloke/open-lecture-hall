17.581 Riots, Rebellions, Revolutions

Roger Petersen · 2013 · free course materials

This course examines different types of violent political conflict. It compares and contrasts several social science approaches (psychological, sociological, and political) and analyzes their ability to explain variation in outbreak, duration and outcome of conflict.

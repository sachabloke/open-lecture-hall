18.02SC Multivariable Calculus

Denis Auroux · 2010 · 37 free video lectures

This course covers differential, integral and vector calculus for functions of more than one variable. These mathematical tools and methods are used extensively in the physical sciences, engineering, economics and computer graphics. The materials have been organized to support independent study.…

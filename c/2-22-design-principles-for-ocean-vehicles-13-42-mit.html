2.22 Design Principles for Ocean Vehicles (13.42)

Alexandra Techet · 2005 · free course materials

The course covers the basic techniques for evaluating the maximum forces and loads over the life of a marine structure or vehicle, so as to be able to design its basic configuration. Loads and motions of small and large structures and their short-term and long-term statistics are studied in detail…

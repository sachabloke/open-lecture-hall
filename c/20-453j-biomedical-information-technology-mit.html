20.453J Biomedical Information Technology

C. Forbes Dewey, Hanry Yu, Sourav Saha Bhowmick · 2008 · free course materials

This course teaches the design of contemporary information systems for biological and medical data. Examples are chosen from biology and medicine to illustrate complete life cycle information systems, beginning with data acquisition, following to data storage and finally to retrieval and analysis.…

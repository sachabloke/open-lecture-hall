21M.342 Composing for Jazz Orchestra

Mark Harvey · 2008 · 7 free video lectures

This class explores composition and arrangement for the large jazz ensemble from 1920s foundations to current postmodern practice. Consideration given to a variety of styles and to the interaction of improvisation and composition. Study of works by Basie, Ellington, Evans, Gillespie, Golson,…

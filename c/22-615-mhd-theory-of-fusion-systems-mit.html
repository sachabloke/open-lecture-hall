22.615 MHD Theory of Fusion Systems

Jeffrey Freidberg · 2007 · free course materials

This course discusses MHD equilibria in cylindrical, toroidal, and noncircular tokamaks. It covers derivation of the basic MHD model from the Boltzmann equation, use of MHD equilibrium theory in poloidal field design, MHD stability theory including the Energy Principle, interchange instability,…

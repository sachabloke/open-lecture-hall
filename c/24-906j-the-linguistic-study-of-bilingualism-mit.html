24.906J The Linguistic Study of Bilingualism

Suzanne Flynn · 2024 · free course materials

One mind, two languages. The basic focus of this class will be on the linguistic and psycholinguistic underpinnings of the bilingual brain, seeking to understand what it means to “acquire” a new language. How did we learn our first language(s)? Were we taught English? Japanese? Chinese?…

24.908 Creole Languages and Caribbean Identities

Michel DeGraff · 2017 · 14 free video lectures

Caribbean Creole languages result from language contact via colonization and the slave trade. In this course we explore the history of Creole languages from cognitive, historical and comparative perspectives. We evaluate popular theories about “Creole genesis” and the role of language acquisition.…

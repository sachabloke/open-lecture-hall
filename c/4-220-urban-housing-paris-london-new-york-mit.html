4.220 Urban Housing: Paris, London, New York

Michael Dennis · 2004 · free course materials

This class presents an analysis of the development of housing models and their urban implications in Paris, London, and New York City from the seventeenth century to the present. The focus will be on three models: the French hotel, the London row house, and the New York City tenement and apartment…

5.07SC Biological Chemistry I

John Essigmann, Joanne Stubbe, Bogdan Fedeles · 2013 · 25 free video lectures

This course examines the chemical and physical properties of the cell and its building blocks, with special emphasis on the structures of proteins and principles of catalysis, as well as the chemistry of organic / inorganic cofactors required for chemical transformations within the cell. Topics…

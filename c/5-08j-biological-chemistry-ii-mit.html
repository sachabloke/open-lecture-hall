5.08J Biological Chemistry II

Joanne Stubbe, Elizabeth Nolan · 2016 · 50 free video lectures

This course is an advanced treatment of biochemical mechanisms that underlie biological processes. Topics include macromolecular machines such as the ribosome, the proteasome, fatty acid synthases as a paradigm for polyketide synthases and non-ribosomal polypeptide synthases, and polymerases.…

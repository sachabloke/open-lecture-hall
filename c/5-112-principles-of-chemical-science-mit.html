5.112 Principles of Chemical Science

Christopher Cummins, Sylvia Ceyer · 2005 · 35 free video lectures

5.112 is an introductory chemistry course for students with an unusually strong background in chemistry. Knowledge of calculus equivalent to MIT course 18.01 is recommended. Emphasis is on basic principles of atomic and molecular electronic structure, thermodynamics, acid-base and redox…

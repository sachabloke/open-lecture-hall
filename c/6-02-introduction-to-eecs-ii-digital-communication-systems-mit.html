6.02 Introduction to EECS II: Digital Communication Systems

Hari Balakrishnan, George Verghese · 2012 · 24 free video lectures

An introduction to several fundamental ideas in electrical engineering and computer science, using digital communication systems as the vehicle. The three parts of the course—bits, signals, and packets—cover three corresponding layers of abstraction that form the basis of communication systems…

6.262 Discrete Stochastic Processes

Robert Gallager · 2011 · 25 free video lectures

Discrete stochastic processes are essentially probabilistic systems that evolve in time via random changes occurring at discrete fixed or random intervals. This course aims to help students acquire both the mathematical principles and the intuition necessary to create, analyze, and understand…

6.849 Geometric Folding Algorithms: Linkages, Origami, Polyhedra

Erik Demaine · 2012 · 39 free video lectures

This course focuses on the algorithms for analyzing and designing geometric foldings. Topics include reconfiguration of foldable structures, linkages made from one-dimensional rods connected by hinges, folding two-dimensional paper (origami), and unfolding and folding three-dimensional polyhedra.…

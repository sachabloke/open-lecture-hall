6.896 Theory of Parallel Hardware (SMA 5511)

Bradley Kuszmaul, Charles Leiserson, Michael Bender · 2004 · free course materials

6.896 covers mathematical foundations of parallel hardware, from computer arithmetic to physical design, focusing on algorithmic underpinnings. Topics covered include: arithmetic circuits, parallel prefix, systolic arrays, retiming, clocking methodologies, boolean logic, sorting networks,…

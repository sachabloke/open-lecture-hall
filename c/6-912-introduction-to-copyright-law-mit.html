6.912 Introduction to Copyright Law

Keith Winstein · 2006 · 4 free video lectures

This course is an introduction to copyright law and American law in general. Topics covered include: structure of federal law; basics of legal research; legal citations; how to use LexisNexis®; the 1976 Copyright Act; copyright as applied to music, computers, broadcasting, and education; fair use;…

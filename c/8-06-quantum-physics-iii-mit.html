8.06 Quantum Physics III

Barton Zwiebach · 2018 · 100 free video lectures

This course is a continuation of 8.05 Quantum Physics II . It introduces some of the important model systems studied in contemporary physics, including two-dimensional electron systems, the fine structure of hydrogen, lasers, and particle scattering. The lectures and lecture notes for this course…

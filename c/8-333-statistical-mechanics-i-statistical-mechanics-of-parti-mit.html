8.333 Statistical Mechanics I: Statistical Mechanics of Particles

Mehran Kardar · 2013 · 26 free video lectures

Statistical Mechanics is a probabilistic approach to equilibrium properties of large numbers of degrees of freedom. In this two-semester course, basic principles are examined. Topics include: Thermodynamics, probability theory, kinetic theory, classical statistical mechanics, interacting systems,…

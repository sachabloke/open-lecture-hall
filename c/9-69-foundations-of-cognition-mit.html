9.69 Foundations of Cognition

Josh Tenenbaum, Lera Boroditsky · 2003 · free course materials

Advances in cognitive science have resolved, clarified, and sometimes complicated some of the great questions of Western philosophy: what is the structure of the world and how do we come to know it; does everyone represent the world the same way; what is the best way for us to act in the world.…

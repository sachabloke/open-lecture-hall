Adsorption Science and Technology: Fundamentals and Applications

Prof. Sourav Mondal · 2025 · 40 free video lectures

The course is designed focusing on the adsorption fundamentals and the application, both at the introductory and advanced level. Adsorption technology is an important separation technology that has wide spread applications from waste water treatment to chromatography, ion exchange, gas separation…

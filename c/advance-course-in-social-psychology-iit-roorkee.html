Advance Course In Social Psychology

Prof. Pooja Garg · 55 free video lectures

This course offers an introduction to classic and contemporary social psychology, covering topics such as interpersonal attraction and understanding of social relationship, attitude, persuasion, interactive behavior, impression management, conflict resolution, and communication

Advanced Distributed systems

Prof. Smruti Ranjan Sarangi · 2025 · 28 free video lectures

This course is on Advanced Distributed Systems. It will start with epidemic and gossip based algorithms and then move on to peer-to-peer networks. The core focus in this part will be on distributed hash tables (DHTs). Then, the course will focus on theoretical aspects such as vector clocks…

Advanced Plasticity of Solid Continua

Prof. Debasish Roy · 72 free video lectures

This graduate course offers a rigorous, field-theoretic treatment of plastic deformation in solid continua, grounded in finite-deformation kinematics and rational thermodynamics. We develop thermodynamically consistent constitutive closures using internal variables and the dissipation inequality…

AI driven Perception, Learning and Mapping for Drones

Prof. Suresh Sundaram,, Prof. Aniruddh Sikdar · 48 free video lectures

This course introduces the fundamental principles of deep learning, from shallow models to advanced neural networks, with a strong focus on aerial perception. Students will learn core CNN architectures, training strategies, and methods for object detection, segmentation, and generative modeling.…

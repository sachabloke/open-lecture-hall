An Introduction to Evolutionary Biology

Prof. Sutirth Dey · 51 free video lectures

This is an introductory course in evolutionary biology. The aim is to give the students an overview of the process of evolution and how it connects various disciplines of biology. We will also look at how evolutionary thinking informs other domains of human interest like medicine, psychology…

An introduction to smooth manifolds

Prof. Harish Seshadri · 68 free video lectures

The goal of this course is to introduce the student to the basics of smooth manifold theory. The course will start with a brief outline of the prerequisites from topology and multi-variable calculus. After the introducion of differentiable manifolds, a large class of examples, including Lie groups…

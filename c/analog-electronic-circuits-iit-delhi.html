Analog Electronic Circuits

Prof. Shouribrata chatterjee · 2024 · 40 free video lectures

This is a basic analog electronics course. The most important objective for electronic circuits is to build an amplifier. This course will develop the principles behind the design of an amplifier. You should be able to design an operational-amplifier independently well before the end of the course.…

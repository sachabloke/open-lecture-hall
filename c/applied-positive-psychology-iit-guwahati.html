Applied Positive Psychology

Prof. Dilwar Hussain · 34 free video lectures

Positive psychology is generally defined as the scientific study of what goes right in life and what makes life most worth living. This field emphasizes using science to understand optimal human functioning. Though it has existed explicitly since 1998, there is now a wealth of research and theory…

Applied Thermodynamics For Engineers

Prof. Dipankar N. Basu · 2023 · 36 free video lectures

Thermodynamics is a subject of fundamental interest to Mechanical engineers and therefore is always taught in the 2nd or 3rd semester. Present course can be viewed as the next step, where the thermodynamic principles will be employed to discuss about different power producing & absorbing cycles.…

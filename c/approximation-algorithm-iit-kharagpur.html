Approximation Algorithm

Prof. Palash Dey · 2025 · 60 free video lectures

Many real-world problems are NP-complete. Hence, they are unlikely to admit a polynomial-time algorithm. In this course, we will study various techniques to design efficient algorithms to compute an approximately optimal solutions.

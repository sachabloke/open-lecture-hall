Aspen Plus simulation software - a basic course for beginners

Prof. Prabirkumar Saha · 35 free video lectures

Aspen Plus is a process modeling tool used for process monitoring, optimization and conceptual design, especially by chemical process industries. This is a simple course on Aspen Plus Simulation engine that will teach one how to model the most common unit operations of a chemical plant. Basic unit…

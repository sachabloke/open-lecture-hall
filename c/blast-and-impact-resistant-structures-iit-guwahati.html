Blast and Impact Resistant Structures

Prof. Hrishikesh Sharma · 60 free video lectures

The course "Blast and Impact Resistant Structures" offers an in-depth exploration of the behavior of structures subjected to extreme events such as blasts and impacts. Students will gain an understanding of key concepts such as quasi-static approximations, stress waves, and energy-absorbing…

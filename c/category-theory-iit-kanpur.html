Category Theory

Prof. Amit Kuber · 24 free video lectures

Category theory is a foundation of mathematics that tries to study structures from an extrinsic viewpoint, i.e., in relation with other structures of similar kinds, just like a society! The internal structure of objects in a category (e.g., elements in a set) is irrelevant if it is not visible…

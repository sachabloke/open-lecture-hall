Centenary celebration of the first modern Spanish endowment at Oxford

2020 · 4 free audio lectures

This podcast series celebrates the centenary of the de Osma Studentship, founded in 1920 by Guillermo de Osma, the first Spaniard to graduate from Oxford after the Universities Tests Act in 1871 opened the ancient universities of England to non-Anglicans.

Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics

Prof. Jayant K. Singh · 2019 · 63 free video lectures

This course covers the foundation of classical thermodynamics in the form of postulates, and later their applications to open and close systems, criteria of stability and equilibria, the equation of states, properties of pure fluids and mixtures, theories and model of phase equilibrium, and…

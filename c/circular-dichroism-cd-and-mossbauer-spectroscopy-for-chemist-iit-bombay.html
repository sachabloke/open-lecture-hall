IIT Bombay · Chemistry

Circular dichroism (CD) and Mossbauer spectroscopy for chemists

Prof. Arnab Dutta · 2025 · 62 free video lectures

In this course, we are going to discuss the fundamentals of two unique spectroscopic techniques: Circular Dichroism (CD) and Mossbauer spectroscopy. These techniques are becoming essential for deciphering the structural dynamics of inorganic complexes in recent times.

CMS.611J Creating Video Games

Philip B. Tan, Richard Eberhardt, Sara Verrilli · 2014 · 26 free video lectures

CMS.611J / 6.073 Creating Video Games is a class that introduces students to the complexities of working in small, multidisciplinary teams to develop video games. Students will learn creative design and production methods, working together in small teams to design, develop, and thoroughly test…

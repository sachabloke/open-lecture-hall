Complex Analysis

Prof. Kaushik Bal · 37 free video lectures

Complex analysis studies holomorphic functions—complex differentiable everywhere—and their remarkable properties on the Riemann sphere. The course begins with Cauchy's theorem and integral formula, enabling powerful contour integration techniques that simplify real integrals via residues. Key…

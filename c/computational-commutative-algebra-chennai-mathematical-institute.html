Computational Commutative Algebra

Prof. Manoj Kummini · 60 free video lectures

This is an introductory course in computational commutative algebra. Topics in a typical first course in commutative algebra are developed along with computations in Macaulay2. The emphasis will be on concrete computations, more than on giving complete proofs of theorems. INTENDED AUDIENCE…

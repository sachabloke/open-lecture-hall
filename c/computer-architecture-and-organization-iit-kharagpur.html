Computer architecture and organization

Prof. Indranil Sengupta, Prof. Kamalika Datta · 2025 · 64 free video lectures

This course will discuss the basic concepts of computer architecture and organization that can help the participants to have a clear view as to how a computer system works. Examples and illustrations will be mostly based on a popular Reduced Instruction Set Computer (RISC) platform. Illustrative…

Computer System Optimizations for ML

Prof. Sumit K. Mandal · 60 free video lectures

Increasing complexity of machine learning (ML) algorithms have necessitated the emergence of specialized computer systems. Some ML algorithms are executed at edge, some are executed on the cloud. In this course, we will delve into different computing kernels of both training and inference of ML…

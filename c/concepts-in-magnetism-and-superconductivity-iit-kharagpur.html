Concepts in Magnetism and Superconductivity

Prof. A Taraphder · 40 free video lectures

The course aims to introduce and work through the underlying concepts behind magnetism and superconductivity. Starting from an electron in a magnetic field, the magnetic responses of a collection of atoms in a solid are worked out. Magnetization and susceptibility in para and diamagnetic cases…

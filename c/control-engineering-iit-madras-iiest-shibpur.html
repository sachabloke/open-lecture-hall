Control engineering

Prof. Mousumi Mukherjee, Prof. Ramkrishna Pasumarthy · 2025 · 55 free video lectures

This course shall introduce the fundamentals of modeling and control of linear time invariant systems; primarily from the classical viewpoint of Laplace transforms and a brief emphasis on the state space formulation as well. The course will be useful for students from major streams of engineering…

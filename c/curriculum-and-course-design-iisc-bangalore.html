Curriculum and Course Design

Prof. K. Rajanikanth,, Prof. N. J. Rao · 36 free video lectures

Fink’s model of teaching strongly suggests that course design has the greatest influence on the quality of learning. There are many Instructional System Design models in use for the design of courses. ADDIE model and its minor variants have been the most extensively used ones for several decades.…

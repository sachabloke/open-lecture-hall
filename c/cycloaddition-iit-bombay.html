Cycloaddition

Prof. Krishna P Kaliappan · 20 free video lectures

In this course, Diels-Alder reaction, 1,3-dipolar cycloaddition, Thermal and photochemical [2+2] cycloaddition reactions followed by synthetic applications of these reactions will be discussed in details.

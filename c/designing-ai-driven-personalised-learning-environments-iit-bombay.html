Designing AI-driven Personalised Learning Environments

Prof. Ramkumar Rajendran · 80 free video lectures

This course introduces the theoretical foundations, computational models, and design principles underlying AI-driven Personalised Adaptive Learning (PAL) systems. The course begins by grounding personalisation in key learning theories, including constructivism, self-regulated learning, cognition…

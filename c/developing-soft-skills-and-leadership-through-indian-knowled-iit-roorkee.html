Developing Soft Skills and Leadership through Indian Knowledge System

Prof. Nagendra Kumar, Prof. Anurag Kumar · 60 free video lectures

This course offers an exploration of modern soft skills through Indian Knowledge Systems, where students will critically reflect on values such as Dharma, social harmony and Indian ethos. The course provides an examination of how ancient insights and concepts can help shape ethical conduct…

Dialogues on Educational Justice: Brought to you by the Repair-Ed project

Claire Neaves · 2026 · 13 free audio lectures

Reparative Futures of Education (Repair-Ed) is a research project that examines race and class injustices in education across the city of Bristol, England, exploring collective forms of redress and reconstruction. For the podcast, the Repair-Ed team talk to people in Bristol, the UK and beyond…

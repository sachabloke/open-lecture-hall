Early Modern England: Politics, Religion, and Society under the Tudors and Stuarts

Keith E. Wrightson · 2009 · 25 free video lectures

This course is intended to provide an up-to-date introduction to the development of English society between the late fifteenth and the early eighteenth centuries. Particular issues addressed in the lectures will include: the changing social structure; households; local communities; gender roles…

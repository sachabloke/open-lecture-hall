Ecology and Society

Prof. Ngamjahao Kipgen · 29 free video lectures

The course focuses on the ecology of human societies –human-environment relationships, with reference to cultural ecology and issues surrounding sustainable development. The ecology of human societies is about connections between ecological and human social, cultural, and organizational processes.…

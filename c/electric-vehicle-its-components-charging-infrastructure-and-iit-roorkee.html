Electric Vehicle: Its Components, Charging Infrastructure and Grid Integration Solution

Prof. Siba Kumar Patro, Prof. Narayana Prasad Padhy, Prof. Ramkrishna Pasumarthy · 59 free video lectures

The course will enable the students to understand the basic concepts of electric vehicles. The course will start with the components in EV power train and their design, which will be followed by the charging infrastructure. The course will also cover the effect of EV on grid.

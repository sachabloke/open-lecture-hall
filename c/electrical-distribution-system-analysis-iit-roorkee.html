Prof. N P Padhy and Late Prof. G. B. Kumbhar · 2023 · 60 free video lectures

This course offers a comprehensive study of electrical distribution systems, emphasizing the fundamental differences from transmission systems in terms of structure and load patterns. With the rapid transition from passive to active networks, driven by the integration of distributed generation…

Electronic Systems Design: Hands-on Circuits and PCB Design with CAD Software

Prof. Ankur Gupta · 36 free video lectures

This course is designed with an aim to make participants familiar with printed circuit board (PCB) design and fundamentals of electronic circuits. Participants will have the capability to examine and build fundamental analog and digital circuits, in addition to creating printed circuit boards…

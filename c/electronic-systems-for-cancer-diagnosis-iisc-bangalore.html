Electronic Systems for Cancer Diagnosis

Prof. Hardik Jeetendra Pandya · 2024 · 71 free video lectures

This course is designed with an aim of educating students on the process flow for designing electronic systems for tissue-based cancer diagnosis. An exposure towards developing strategies to discriminate pathological cells from normal ones based on the electrophysiological properties of cells.…

Elements Of Mechanical Vibration

Prof. Ashish K Darpe · 46 free video lectures

It is hard to imagine any mechanical/aerospace system that is not subjected to dynamic excitation force. The time-varying forces excite these machines/structures and manifests in their vibratory motion. The vibratory motion greatly influences the stresses and hence the durability and residual life…

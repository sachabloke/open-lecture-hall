Enhanced Oil Recovery Techniques

Prof. Ajay Mandal · 60 free video lectures

Enhanced Oil Recovery (EOR) methods aim to improve oil extraction efficiency. The mechanisms involve altering the mobility ratio, reducing interfacial tension, changing wettability, lowering oil viscosity, and forming oil banks. Chemical, Miscible, and Thermal are three main EOR methods widely used…

ES.260 Women's Novels: A Weekly Book Club

Faye Kasemset, Holly Sweet, Jocelyn Rodal · 2006 · free course materials

This pass/fail seminar should be a fun setting where we can all enjoy a love of good books together. Students will read approximately one novel every two weeks, and the class will discuss each novel in a relaxed and interactive setting, with attention to whatever themes and issues interest them…

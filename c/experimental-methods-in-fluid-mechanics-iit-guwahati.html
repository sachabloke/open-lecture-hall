Experimental Methods in Fluid Mechanics

Prof. Pranab K. Mondal · 2025 · 43 free video lectures

This course deals with the experimental techniques in Fluid Mechanics. One part of the course focuses on different techniques and challenges associated with the measurement of flow features. Other part of the course has emphasis on the statistical analysis of experimental data. Thus, this course…

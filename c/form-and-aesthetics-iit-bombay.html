Form and Aesthetics

Prof. B.K. Chakravarthy · 31 free video lectures

The course on Form and aesthetics is a unique course where we are showcasing how the form of a product is generated. We have documented video case studies starting from creating abstract shapes and later on moving to targeted emotive forms. Finally, we have industry case studies of form generation…

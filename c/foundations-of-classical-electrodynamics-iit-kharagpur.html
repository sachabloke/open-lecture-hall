Foundations of Classical Electrodynamics

Prof. Samudra Roy · 73 free video lectures

The course offers a profound understanding of classical electrodynamics where all the problems are solved with mathematical rigor. This self-contained course begins with useful mathematical preliminaries where the students will learn from scratch the mathematical techniques used in electrodynamics.…

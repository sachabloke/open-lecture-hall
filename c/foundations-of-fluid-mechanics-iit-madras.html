Foundations of Fluid Mechanics

Prof. Anubhab Roy · 43 free video lectures

Fluid mechanics lies at the heart of how the natural and engineered world moves — from air flowing over aircraft wings to blood circulating through arteries. This course develops a fundamental understanding of fluid motion and the governing laws that describe it. Starting from the continuum…

Foundations of Immersive Design for AR/VR

Prof. Amar Kumar Behera · 60 free video lectures

This course introduces students to the principles, tools, and design methodologies for Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR). It focuses on designing immersive, interactive, and human-centered experiences by integrating spatial computing, perception, interaction design, and emerging XR…

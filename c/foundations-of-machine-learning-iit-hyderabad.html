Foundations of Machine Learning

Prof. J. SakethaNath · 61 free video lectures

This is a course on Machine Learning that introduces the subject in a formal way with a focus on basic and foundational concepts. A holistic view of machine learning is taken, including aspects of optimization (SGD) that are ignored in classical narratives. Topics that are relevant to modern models…

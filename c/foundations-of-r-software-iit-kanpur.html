Foundations of R Software

Prof. Shalabh · 2023 · 54 free video lectures

Any scientific task without the knowledge of software is difficult to complete in the current scenario. R is free software that is capable of mathematical and statistical computing. It has its own programming language, built-in functions, and graphical capabilities to perform any specialized task…

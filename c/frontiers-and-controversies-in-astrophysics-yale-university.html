Frontiers and Controversies in Astrophysics

Charles Bailyn · 2007 · 29 free video lectures

This course focuses on three particularly interesting areas of astronomy that are advancing very rapidly: Extra-Solar Planets, Black Holes, and Dark Energy. Particular attention is paid to current projects that promise to improve our understanding significantly over the next few years. The course…

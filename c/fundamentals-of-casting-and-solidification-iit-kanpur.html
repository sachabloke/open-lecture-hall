Fundamentals of Casting and Solidification

Prof. Virkeshwar Kumar · 60 free video lectures

This course, Fundamentals of Casting and Solidification, offers an in-depth understanding of the principles governing metal casting processes, from melt flow and heat transfer to solidification dynamics. It integrates heat, mass, and fluid flow analysis with the study of microstructural evolution…

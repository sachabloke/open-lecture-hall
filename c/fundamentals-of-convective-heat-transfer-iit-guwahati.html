Fundamentals of Convective Heat Transfer

Prof. Amaresh Dalal · 2023 · 43 free video lectures

Convective heat transfer is one of the most important areas of engineering sciences. It is major mode of heat transfer during flowing fluid and it is the most common mode of heat transfer used in industry.

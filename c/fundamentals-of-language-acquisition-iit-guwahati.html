Fundamentals of Language Acquisition

Prof. Bidisha Som · 60 free video lectures

How humans acquire language is not a question only linguists ask; it is a mystery to many as how children learn the nuances of the most complex grammatical structures effortlessly that adults and machines find difficult. This is a key topic within Linguistics and all students of the discipline…

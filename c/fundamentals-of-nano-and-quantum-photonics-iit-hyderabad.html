Fundamentals of Nano and Quantum Photonics

Prof. Naresh Kumar Emani · 2024 · 64 free video lectures

The course objective is to familiarize students to the research frontiers in Nano and Quantum Optics. The course will discuss fundamental principles with emphasis on developing intuitive understanding and developing analytical techniques. This course is primarily designed for post-graduate and PhD…

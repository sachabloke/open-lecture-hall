Fundamentals of Physics I

Ramamurti Shankar · 2006 · 25 free video lectures

This course provides a thorough introduction to the principles and methods of physics for students who have good preparation in physics and mathematics. Emphasis is placed on problem solving and quantitative reasoning. This course covers Newtonian mechanics, special relativity, gravitation…

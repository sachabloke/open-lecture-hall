General Pharmacotherapeutics

Prof. Vinod Tiwari · 59 free video lectures

This course General Pharmacotherapeutics provides a comprehensive and clinically oriented understanding of drug therapy for major human diseases . The course integrates pathophysiology, clinical features, rational pharmacological management, non-pharmacological interventions, and patient counseling…

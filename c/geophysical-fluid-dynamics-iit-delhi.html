Geophysical Fluid Dynamics

Prof. Ritabrata Thakur · 59 free video lectures

This course on geophysical fluid dynamics introduces advanced undergraduates and postgraduates to the phenomena of oceanic and atmospheric flows. It starts with the basic governing equations in the rotating frame of reference. We then discuss simplified models for geophysical flows and dispersion…

German - III

Prof. Milind Brahme · 2019 · 68 free video lectures

German 3 will build upon the courses German 1 and German 2, which are already being offered on the NPTEL platform since two years. The overall objective of this course is to acquire good knowledge of the German language, corresponding to the B1 proficiency level of the European Common Framework…

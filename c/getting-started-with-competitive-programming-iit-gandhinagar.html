Getting Started with Competitive Programming

Prof. Neeldhara Misra · 2025 · 54 free video lectures

This is a course on algorithm design with a focus on issues of modeling and implementation. Each lecture will be focused entirely on one or two problems that reveal the use of a specific algorithmic technique. The techniques themselves are chosen to be in line with those covered in existing NPTEL…

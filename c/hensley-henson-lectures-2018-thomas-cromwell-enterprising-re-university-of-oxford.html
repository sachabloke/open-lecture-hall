Hensley Henson Lectures 2018 - Thomas Cromwell: Enterprising Reformation

Diarmaid MacCulloch · 2018 · 4 free video lectures

The Hensley Henson lectures for 2017-18 are based on Prof. MacCulloch's six-year-long project of writing a new life of Thomas Cromwell. Grounded in a new examination of Cromwell's vast and complex archive, they reassess the religion and religious policies of the man at the heart of the revolution…

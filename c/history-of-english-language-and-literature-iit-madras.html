History of English Language and Literature

Prof. Merin Simi Raj · 2024 · 65 free video lectures

This course is a chronological survey of the major forces and voices that have contributed to the development of an English literary tradition. It intends to cover the literary ground from the Old English Period till the mid twentieth century focusing on the emergence, evolution and progress of…

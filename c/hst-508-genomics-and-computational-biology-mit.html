HST.508 Genomics and Computational Biology

George Church · 2002 · 21 free audio lectures

This course will assess the relationships among sequence, structure, and function in complex biological networks as well as progress in realistic modeling of quantitative, comprehensive, functional genomics analyses. Exercises will include algorithmic, statistical, database, and simulation…

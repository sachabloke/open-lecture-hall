HST.512 Genomic Medicine

Isaac Kohane · 2004 · 19 free audio lectures

This course reviews the key genomic technologies and computational approaches that are driving advances in prognostics, diagnostics, and treatment. Throughout the semester, emphasis will return to issues surrounding the context of genomics in medicine including: what does a physician need to know?…

Hydrogen Energy: Production, Storage, Transportation and Safety

Prof. Pratibha Sharma · 2024 · 69 free video lectures

The course will comprehensively cover all the aspects of the hydrogen energy value chain including production methods from hydrocarbons & renewables, separation & purification, storage, transportation & distribution, refueling, utilization in various sectors, associated energy conversion devices…

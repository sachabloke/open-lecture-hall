Indian Art: Materials, Techniques and Artistic Practices

Prof. Rajarshi Sengupta · 2024 · 60 free video lectures

This course introduces the diversity of art practices and material culture from the Indian subcontinent and situates them against their socio-cultural, political and temporal settings. The content is arranged according to materials and techniques to support an immersive approach to understanding…

Indian Society: Sociological Perspectives

Prof. R. Santhosh · 60 free video lectures

This course aims to provide a broad overview of the multiple theoretical and methodological perspectives adopted by sociologists to understand Indian society. It provides a historical overview of the discipline, major perspectives such as Indological, Structural-functionalist, Marxian…

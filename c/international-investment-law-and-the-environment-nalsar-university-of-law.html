International Investment Law and the Environment

Prof. Eluckiaa A · 169 free video lectures

The countries started signing Bilateral Investment Treaties (BITs) and other Treaties with Investment Provisions (TIPs) to afford protections to foreign investment. But over time, these protections given in the BITs and TIPs were invoked to challenge the regulatory measures of the host state taken…

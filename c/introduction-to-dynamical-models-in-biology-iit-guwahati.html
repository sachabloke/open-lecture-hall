Introduction to Dynamical Models in Biology

Prof. Biplab Bose · 24 free video lectures

Mathematical modeling has become an integral part of different fields of biology, from ecology to cell biology. This course will introduce students of biology to elementary mathematical concepts and tools for dynamical models. The course will focus on modeling using ordinary differential equations…

Prof. Parasar Mohanty · 60 free video lectures

This course will cover the fundamental theory of Fourier series and Fourier integrals, as well as Fourier series for finite abelian groups. Some applications of Fourier series in differential equations, the isoperimetric problem, and number theory will be explored. Basic calculus and Riemann…

Laser-Assisted Measurements and Micro Manufacturing

Prof.Nilesh J Vasa, Prof.Palani Anand Iyamperumal · 61 free video lectures

Lasers are widely used in systems for micromanufacturing, medical applications, nanotechnology, communications, environmental monitoring, and various other applications. Laser-Assisted Measurements and Micro Manufacturing is an interdisciplinary course designed to provide an understanding of lasers…

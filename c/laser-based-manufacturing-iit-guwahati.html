Laser Based Manufacturing

Prof. Shrikrishna N. Joshi · 2025 · 29 free video lectures

This is a basic course on applications of laser technology in manufacturing. The subject laser technology has a very wide range of applications in the product development, manufacturing, surface engineering, and instrumentation.

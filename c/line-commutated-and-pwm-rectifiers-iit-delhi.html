Line Commutated and PWM Rectifiers

Prof. Anandarup Das · 32 free video lectures

The course covers different types of AC-DC converters. The technology of diode and thyristor based rectifiers is quite matured. However, they are slowly getting replaced by PWM converters for newer applications like EV and battery charging with improvement in size and efficiency of the converter.…

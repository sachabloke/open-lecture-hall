Linear Algebra Through Geometry

Prof. Arulalan M R, Prof. Ashok Rao, Prof. M Krishna Kumar · 62 free video lectures

This course will provide a holistic approach to LA covering both the algebraic and more importantly, geometric perspectives. A typical linear algebra course focuses on explaining the action of a matrix in abstract or algebraic language. However, we know that a picture is worth a thousand words.…

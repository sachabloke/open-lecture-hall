Linguistic Typology Through Indian Languages

Prof. Bornini Lahiri, Prof. Dripta Piplai (Mondal) · 40 free video lectures

Languages are diverse, however the features which make them different are not innumerable. These features are called parameters. Typology aims to look for those (limited) parameters.

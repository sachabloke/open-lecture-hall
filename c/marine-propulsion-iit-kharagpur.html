Marine Propulsion

Prof. Anirban Bhattacharyya · 2025 · 40 free video lectures

The course covers different aspects of marine propellers and propulsion starting with the basic concepts of screw propeller geometry, theories of propeller action, hydrodynamics in open water and behind-hull conditions and practical calculations of ship powering from model test results. Other…

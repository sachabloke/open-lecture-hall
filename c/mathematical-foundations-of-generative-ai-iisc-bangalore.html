Mathematical Foundations of Generative AI

Prof. Prathosh A. P · 44 free video lectures

This course provides an in-depth exploration of deep generative models, including their probabilistic foundations and learning algorithms. Students will learn about various types of deep generative models such as variational autoencoders, generative adversarial networks, autoregressive models…

Mathematical Methods and its Applications

Prof. P.N. Agarwal, Prof. S. K. Gupta · 2018 · 60 free video lectures

This course is a basic course offered to UG student of Engineering/Science background. It contains ODE,PDE, Laplace transforms, Z-transforms, Fourier series and Fourier transforms. It plays an important role for solving various engineering sciences problems.Therefore, it has tremendous applications…

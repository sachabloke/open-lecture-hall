Matlab-Based Programming Lab In Chemical Engineering

Prof. Parag A. Deshpande · 60 free video lectures

This is computer lab course for the undergraduate students of chemical engineering. We will be using MATLAB as the language for solving chemical engineering problems which the students encounter not only in their undergraduate curriculum but are also relevant and important in process industries.…

Mechanics of Human Movement

Prof. Sujatha Srinivasan · 50 free video lectures

This course deals with applying principles of mechanics to movements of the human body. Understanding the biomechanics of human movement is necessary for the prevention and treatment of musculoskeletal injuries and design of aids for rehabilitation.

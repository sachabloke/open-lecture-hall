Mechanism and Robot Kinematics

Prof. Anirvan Dasgupta · 2019 · 40 free video lectures

This course will be a foundation course in analysis of mechanisms and robots. After a brief introduction to the subject matter and terms, the audience will be introduced to kinematic analysis of planar constrained mechanisms, and closed and open chain robot manipulators.

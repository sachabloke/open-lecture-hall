Microsensors and Nanosensors

Prof. Ravindra Kumar Jha · 60 free video lectures

The course is a basic course on microsensors and nanosensors. The students will be introduced to basic understanding of sensors and interface circuit. The course will start with classification of sensors and its characteristics such as transfer function, hysteresis, nonlinearity, accuracy etc.…

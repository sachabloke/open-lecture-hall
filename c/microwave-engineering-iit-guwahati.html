Microwave Engineering

Prof. Ratnajit Bhattacharjee · 2024 · 37 free video lectures

This course is indented to provide a foundation for microwave engineering to the undergraduate students. Rigorous treatment of the fundamentals of microwave engineering will be provided. Design of different passive and some active microwave circuits/subsystems will be covered in detail. This course…

Nanobiophotonics: Touching Our Daily Life

Prof. Basudev Lahiri · 2025 · 60 free video lectures

Nanobiophotonics is a multidisciplinary field where light-based technologies are utilized to reveal biological mechanisms, and diagnose several diseases along with finding their treatments.

Network Theory

Prof. Shouri Chatterjee · 2019 · 26 free video lectures

The course will guide a student through circuit theory from KCL-KVL to elementary network synthesis. This course will be run with a focus on understanding through practice; extensive MATLAB exercises will support the course. The course will completely cover the AICTE EC-06 syllabus.

Neuroscience of Human Movements

Prof. Varadhan · 2019 · 93 free video lectures

Neuroscience of Human Movement will focus on the neural system responsible for movement generation, movement control and learning of actions. The course will start from the very basics of neuroscience and build theory to understand the movement control system in relatively good detail and depth.…

Novel Drug Delivery Systems

Prof. Sathish Dyawanapelly · 60 free video lectures

This course will provide the understanding of design, development and evaluation of various Novel Drug Delivery Systems (NDDS) includes, nanocarriers (polymeric, lipid, metal), controlled drug delivery systems, targeted delivery, mucosal delivery, implantable delivery, transdermal delivery…

Numerical methods

Prof. Ameeya Kumar Nayak, Prof. Sanjeev Kumar · 2023 · 40 free video lectures

This course is a basic course offered to UG student of Engineering/Science background. It contains solution of system of linear equations, roots of non-linear equations, interpolation, numerical differentiation and integration. It plays an important role for solving various engineering sciences…

Nutritional Epidemiology: A Way Towards a Healthy Life

Prof. Dhananjay D Mankar · 60 free video lectures

This course introduces principles, methods, and applications of nutritional epidemiology — the study of dietary exposures and nutritional status in populations and their relation to health and disease.

Optical Methods for Solid and Fluid Mechanics

Prof. Aloke Kumar, Prof. Koushik Viswanathan · 43 free video lectures

Course dealing with use of optical methods for quantification of fluid and solid mechanics phenomena. On the fluid mechanics front, the course will focus primarily on fluid flow visualization and quantification. Techniques discussed will include shadowgraphy, particle tracking velocimetry (PTV) and…

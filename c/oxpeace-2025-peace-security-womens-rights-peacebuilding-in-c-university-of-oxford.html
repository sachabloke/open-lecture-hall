OxPeace 2025: Peace, Security, Women’s Rights, & Peacebuilding in Challenging Times

2026 · 10 free audio lectures

This OxPeace annual conference focused on developments and challenges in women's involvement in peacebuilding at all levels, assessing progress since UN SC Resolution 1325 (2000) on Women, Peace & Security (WPS) and the Beijing Declaration (1995) on women’s rights.

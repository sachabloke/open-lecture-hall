Partial Differential Equations

Prof. Sivaji Ganesh · 60 free video lectures

Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) appear as mathematical models for many a physical phenomena. Closed-form solutions to most of these PDEs cannot be found. One of the possible ways to understand the models is by studying the qualitative properties exhibited by their solutions. In this course…

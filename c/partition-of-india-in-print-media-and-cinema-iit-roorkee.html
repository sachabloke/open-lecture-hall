Partition of India in Print Media and Cinema

Prof. Sarbani Banerjee · 2024 · 62 free video lectures

The course will acquaint students with the major literary works and cinema that were created in the wake of the Partition of India (1947). It looks into the ways Partition is captured through feminist interpretations by different authors and historians. The gamut of artworks selected for the course…

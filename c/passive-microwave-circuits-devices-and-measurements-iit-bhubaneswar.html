Passive Microwave Circuits, Devices, and Measurements

Prof. Debapratim Ghosh · 60 free video lectures

This course intends to mold good microwave engineers with a skillset that should encompass strong theoretical knowledge, together with practical-oriented knowledge in passive microwave circuits, devices, and measurement techniques. This course will help you find answers to interesting questions…

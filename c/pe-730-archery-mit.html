PE.730 Archery

Cheryl Silva, Jaroslav Koniusz · 2006 · free lectures

This 12 session course is designed for the beginning or novice archer and uses recurve indoor target bows and equipment. The purpose of the course is to introduce students to the basic techniques of indoor target archery emphasizing the care and use of equipment, range safety, stance and shooting…

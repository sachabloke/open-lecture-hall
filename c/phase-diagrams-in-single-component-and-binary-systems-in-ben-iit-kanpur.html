Phase Diagrams in Single Component and Binary Systems in Bengali

Prof. Kallol Mondal · 45 free video lectures

Phase diagram is the basis for understanding the behavior of materials under equilibrium. It has its importance in deciding the phase evolution with the variation of pressure, temperature and composition. Moreover, it helps in understanding the processing of the materials, mainly heat treatment.…

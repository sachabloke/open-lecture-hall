Physics of Renewable Energy Systems

Prof. Amreesh Chandra · 2024 · 51 free video lectures

For a country like India, renewable energy will play an important in ensuring energy safety, security and sustainability. With the fast growing demand in off-grid applications in areas extending from villages to hills, newer technologies will have to be MADE IN INDIA. We will start with the basics…

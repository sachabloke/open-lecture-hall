Polymers: concepts, properties, uses and sustainability

Prof. Abhijit P Deshpande · 2023 · 87 free video lectures

Polymers in solar cells, rubbers in soft robot, polymer composite in ISRO launch vehicle –these are some examples of cutting edge applications of polymeric materials. On the other hand, we see other examples of polymeric materials such as plastics and fiber reinforced plastics (FRP) all around us.…

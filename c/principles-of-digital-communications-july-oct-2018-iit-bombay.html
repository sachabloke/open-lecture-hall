Principles of Digital Communications (July-Oct 2018)

Prof. S. N. Merchant · 65 free video lectures

The course is an introduction to modern digital communications at a senior undergraduate level. The primary goal of the course is to provide the student an understanding of the principles, techniques, trade-offs, and fundamental limits in modern digital communication systems.

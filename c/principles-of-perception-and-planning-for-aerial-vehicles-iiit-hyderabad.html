Principles of Perception and Planning for Aerial Vehicles

Prof. K Madhava Krishna, Dr. Harikumar Kandath, Dr. Antony Thomas · 52 free video lectures

This course provides the details of the important principles of perception and motion planning for aerial vehicles. They form the foundation of autonomous UAV navigation in the presence of obstacles. The first module of the course provides the fundaments of UAV modeling and mathematical tools…

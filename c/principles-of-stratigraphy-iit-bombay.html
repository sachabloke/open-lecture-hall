Principles of Stratigraphy

Prof. Soumyajit Mukherjee · 60 free video lectures

Stratigraphy deals mainly with correlation of layered rocks. In this course, we will learn different principles of such correlation for marine and non-marine rocks in local to regional scales. The subject is of paramount importance in resource geoscience and has tremendous industrial applications…

Proteins and Gel-Based Proteomics

Prof. Sanjeeva Srivastava · 25 free video lectures

This course introduces to the basic biology of proteins and gel-based proteomics. To obtain better understanding of cellular processes & regulation, there has been an increasing interest in studying proteomics. Proteomics techniques aim to look into the protein properties from a global perspective…

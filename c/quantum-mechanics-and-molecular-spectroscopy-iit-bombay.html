Quantum Mechanics and Molecular Spectroscopy

Prof. G Naresh Patwari · 2023 · 38 free video lectures

This course is based on application of quantum mechanics to molecular systems to probe their energy levels. Prior understanding of solutions to the time-independent Schrodinger equation is assumed.

Reagents In Organic Synthesis

Prof. Subhas Chandra Pan · 2019 · 32 free video lectures

This course will deal with the various synthetic strategies using organic reagents. Both classical and modern reagents shall be discussed emphasizing on the mechanistic details. This course shall useful to students of undergraduate, post graduate and Ph.D. Students preparing for NET and GATE…

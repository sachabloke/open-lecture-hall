Regeneration Biology

Prof. Rajesh Ramachandran · 62 free video lectures

This course is aimed at gaining knowledge on the mechanisms of tissue regeneration on various animals. Regeneration is a way of living in some group of animals such as planaria and zebrafish, where as it is highly limited to a few tissue types in mammals. Knowledge on the mechanisms of regeneration…

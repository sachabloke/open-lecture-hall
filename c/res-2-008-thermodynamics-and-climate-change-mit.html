RES.2-008 Thermodynamics and Climate Change

Peter Godart · 2022 · free course materials

In this course you will learn the three laws of thermodynamics, explore concepts like entropy and enthalpy, and investigate the causes and effects of global warming from a thermodynamics perspective. We will also apply these concepts to learning about state-of-the-art energy conversion and storage…

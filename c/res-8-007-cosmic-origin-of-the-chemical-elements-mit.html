RES.8-007 Cosmic Origin of the Chemical Elements

Anna Frebel · 2019 · 11 free video lectures

Everything around us is made from different chemical elements: carbon, silicon, iron, and all the other elements from the Periodic Table. The lighter elements were mostly produced in the Big Bang, but the rest were (and are) formed within stars and in the explosions of supernovae. In this series…

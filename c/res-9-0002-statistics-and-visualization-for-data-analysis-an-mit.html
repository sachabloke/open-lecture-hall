RES.9-0002 Statistics and Visualization for Data Analysis and Inference

Ed Vul, Mike Frank · 2009 · free course materials

A whirl-wind tour of the statistics used in behavioral science research, covering topics including: data visualization, building your own null-hypothesis distribution through permutation, useful parametric distributions, the generalized linear model, and model-based analyses more generally.…

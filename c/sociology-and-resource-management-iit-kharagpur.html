Sociology and Resource Management

Prof. Amrita Sen, Prof. Archana Patnaik · 2023 · 20 free video lectures

The aim of this course is to bring in various sociological dimensions of managing and governing resources be it a natural resource such as land, water, fisheries, forest or a man-made resource like internet, knowledge, culture, etc. The course will reflect on different perspectives on conservation…

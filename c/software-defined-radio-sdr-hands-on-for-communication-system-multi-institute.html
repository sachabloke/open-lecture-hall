Software-Defined Radio (SDR) Hands-on for Communication Systems Design

Prof. Suraj Srivastava · 49 free video lectures

In an era of rapidly evolving wireless communications and digital signal processing, industry and academia demand engineers who can design, simulate and implement communication systems with confidence. This intensive course aims to bridge the gap between theory and practice by providing hands-on…

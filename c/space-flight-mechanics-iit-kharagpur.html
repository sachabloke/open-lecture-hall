Space Flight Mechanics

Prof. Manoranjan Sinha · 2024 · 99 free video lectures

This course is designed to introduce orbital mechanics of satellite. The course will begin with central force motion and then proceed to the two- body and three-body dynamics under mutual gravitational acceleration. It will also introduce the concept of Lagrange Points and their stability.…

Stress and Strain Issues in Structural Geology

Prof. Soumyajit Mukherjee · 60 free video lectures

Stress and strain concepts will be explained in structural geological contexts. Several interesting and easy to follow applications will be presented that are otherwise not possible to cover during tight schedule of semesters.

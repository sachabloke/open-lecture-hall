Sustainable Power Generation Systems

Prof. Pankaj Kalita · 37 free video lectures

The course content is designed to provide comprehensive knowledge of various renewable energy systems. Specifically, in this course, the design and analysis of renewable energy power plants will be discussed. The concepts will be illustrated with practical examples, schematics and block diagrams…

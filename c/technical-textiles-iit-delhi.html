Technical Textiles

Prof. Apurba Das · 2024 · 40 free video lectures

Technical textiles are defined as textile materials and products used primarily for their technical performance and functional properties rather than their aesthetic or decorative characteristics. This course targets the specific areas of technical textiles depending on the product characteristics…

The Fiction of Indian Diaspora

Prof. Nagendra Kumar · 60 free video lectures

The course “The Fiction of Indian Diaspora” primarily deals with Diaspora, Indian diasporic fictions and the theorisation of Diaspora. It provides an in-depth exploration of the concept of Diaspora, tracing its origins from the Greek term diaspeirein (“to scatter”) to its contemporary formations…

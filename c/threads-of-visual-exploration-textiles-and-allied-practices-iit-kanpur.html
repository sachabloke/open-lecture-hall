Threads of Visual Exploration: Textiles and Allied Practices - Part II

Prof. Rajarshi Sengupta · 56 free video lectures

This course puts forward an invested and interdisciplinary way of examining, analysing and appreciating Indian textiles drawing on the ongoing scholarly discussions around craft-art interface, visual and contextual analysis of art practices and sustainability.

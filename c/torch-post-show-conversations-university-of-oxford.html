TORCH Post-Show Conversations

Ruth Moore · 2022 · 5 free audio lectures

A series of informal, ‘on location’ conversations between Oxford researchers in response to a current theatre production. Designed to capture 'on the night' responses to performance, the Conversations give listeners a chance to eavesdrop on the intersections between research and theatre practice…

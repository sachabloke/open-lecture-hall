Tribal Studies in India: Interdisciplinary Perspectives and Approaches

Prof. Roluahpuia, Prof. Sarbani Banerjee · 64 free video lectures

The course familiarizes students with the concepts and approaches to the study of tribal communities in India from an interdisciplinary perspective. It offers foundational understanding of the existing theories and important readings of this field of study. It provides case studies from different…

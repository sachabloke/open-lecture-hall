Ventilation and Climate Control

Prof. Paluchamy B · 60 free video lectures

The course “Ventilation and Climate Control” focuses on the principles and practices of controlling air quality, heat, and environmental conditions in underground mines. It covers psychrometry, human thermoregulation, heat, dust, gases in mines, air cooling systems, airflow mechanics, mine fans…

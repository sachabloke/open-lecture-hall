Wave Optics

Prof. Samudra Roy · 2025 · 66 free video lectures

The course offers a profound understanding of the wave nature of light. This self-contained course begins with useful preliminaries related to the wave propagation and electromagnetic theory of light. Then the fundamental concept of various phenomenon like diffraction, interference and polarization…

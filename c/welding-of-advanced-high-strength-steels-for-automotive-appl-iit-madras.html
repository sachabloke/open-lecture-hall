Welding of Advanced High Strength Steels for Automotive Applications

Prof. Murugaiyan Amirthalingam · 2019 · 20 free video lectures

The use of advanced high strength steels (AHSS) is increasingly preferred in automotive applications due to improved crash energy management and enhanced strength-ductility combinations, resulting in greener and safer vehicles. The weldability of AHSS is generally poorer than conventional steels…

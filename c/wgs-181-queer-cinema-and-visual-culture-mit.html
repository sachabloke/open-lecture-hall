WGS.181 Queer Cinema and Visual Culture

K.J. Surkan · 2017 · free course materials

This course analyzes mainstream, popular films produced in the post-World War II 20th century U.S. as cultural texts that shed light on ongoing historical struggles over gender identity and appropriate sexual behaviors. It traces the history of LGBTQ/queer film through the 20th and into the 21st…

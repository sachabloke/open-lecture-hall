WGS.225J Science of Race, Sex, and Gender

Abha Sur Abha Sur, Stefan Helmreich · 2023 · free course materials

This course examines the role of science and medicine in the origins and evolution of concepts of race, sex, and gender from the 17th century to the present. We analyze biological, medical, and anthropological studies and how they intersect with historical, social, political, and cultural ideas…

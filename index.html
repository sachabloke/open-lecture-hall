About this site

Free lecture recordings are disappearing. Universities move platforms, close channels and delete old webcasts. This site gathers the courses that are still free to watch or listen to, and points to Internet Archive copies where they exist so they stay findable.

Everything listed is free to use without an account. Certificates and graded work on some platforms cost money; the lectures themselves do not. Podcast feeds work in any podcast app: copy the feed link and paste it in as "add by URL".
